Weight-bearing movement keeps bone tissue dense — especially important in perimenopause.

Why this matters ›
Bone density peaks in your mid-30s and slowly declines after. Brisk walking or light resistance training — even 15–20 min a few times a week — stimulates the cells that keep bone strong. Bone loss has no symptoms until much later, so it's easy to miss.
